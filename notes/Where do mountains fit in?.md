Mountains are an evidence of the past. They carry the wounds and markings of rough weathers, geological and other atmospheric changes that they have witnessed. Today mountains are not just objects of fear and fascination but of man's detour from Nature, into a nature of its own making. Man's mountains are not just the witnessed, photographed, climbed, or the inhabited ones, but also the 'tamed' ones. For man believes mountainous terrain to be unruly. A disoriented animal roaming in the wild that the man has set his mind upon to domesticate. Mountain roads are the manifestation of this project. Hills are cut with man's machine-powered might, and from there is laid the dark long blanket of asphalt, comforting the distance into submission.
